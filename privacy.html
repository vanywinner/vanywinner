Privacy Policy

How Vanywinner News collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who we are

Vanywinner News ("we", "us") is an independent news website at vanywinner.co.ke, run from Nairobi, Kenya by Okhala Winslaus Ondwasi. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the site, why, and what choices you have. For any privacy question, email vanywinner@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Messages you send us. If you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message. If you email or WhatsApp us, we receive whatever you choose to send.
  • Technical data. When you visit, our hosting provider may record your IP address, browser type, device, the pages you request and the time of the visit. This is standard for any website.
  • Analytics data (only if you accept cookies). Pages viewed, approximate location, device type and how you arrived, collected by Google Analytics.
  • Your cookie choice. We save your answer on your device so we do not ask again on every page.

You do not need an account to read Vanywinner News. We do not ask for or store payment details.

3. How we use information

  • To run, secure and improve the website.
  • To understand which stories readers find useful, so we can report better.
  • To reply to your messages and corrections requests.
  • To show advertising, where we choose to carry it (see section 6).
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Our basis for using information

We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. We rely on your consent for analytics and advertising cookies, which you can withdraw at any time through "Cookie settings" in the footer. We rely on our legitimate interest in running and protecting the site, and on the need to respond when you contact us.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers, who process information on our behalf:

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the website and may log visits.
  • Cloudflare manages our domain name records.
  • Google provides Google Analytics (only after you accept), Google Fonts (which loads the site's typefaces) and YouTube (for videos you choose to play). Google may also provide advertising, see section 6.
  • FormSubmit delivers contact form messages to our email inbox.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

6. Advertising (Google AdSense)

We may show advertisements from Google AdSense. Where ads appear, Google and its advertising partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. This lets Google and its partners show ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of personalised ads from other advertising companies.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technology/partner-sites.

Where the law requires consent for personalised advertising, we will ask for it first.

7. Cookies

Our Cookie Policy lists the cookies and similar technologies we use and how to control them.

8. Transfers outside Kenya

Some of our providers, including Google and GitHub, process information on servers outside Kenya, including in the United States. By using the site you understand that your information may be processed there under those providers' own safeguards.

9. How long we keep information

We keep messages for as long as needed to reply and keep a record of corrections, then delete them when you ask or when they are no longer needed. Server logs and analytics data are kept for the periods set by the providers above.

10. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 you have the right to be informed about how your data is used, to access it, to ask us to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw your consent. To use any of these rights, email vanywinner@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at odpc.go.ke.

11. Children

Vanywinner News is a general news site and is not aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold.

13. Links to other sites

Our stories and pages link to other websites, including social media and news sources. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make important changes, we will say so on the site.

15. Contact

Vanywinner News, Nairobi, Kenya. Email vanywinner@gmail.com.
