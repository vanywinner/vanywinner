About Vanywinner News

An independent news site from Nairobi, bringing readers clear, fairly reported stories from Kenya, Africa and the world.

What we do

Vanywinner News covers news, business and sports, with a growing focus on data and the economy. We publish at vanywinner.co.ke and aim to make the day's important stories easy to read on any phone.

The site is run by one person, which means every story is written, checked and posted by the same hand. We would rather publish fewer stories that we can stand behind than rush out many we cannot.

Our standards

  • Original writing. We write our own reports and do not copy other outlets' articles. When we rely on another source, we say so.
  • Clear attribution. Claims, especially serious allegations, are attributed to the person or document they come from and are not presented as established fact.
  • Corrections. If we get something wrong, tell us at vanywinner@gmail.com and we will correct the story and note the change.

About the founder

Okhala Winslaus Ondwasi is a data analyst and statistics teacher. He is a final-year BSc Applied Statistics with Information Technology student at Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology, with graduation expected in December 2026.

He completed a three-month industrial attachment as a statistical analyst at the Public Benefit Organizations Regulatory Authority (PBORA). He also builds websites, including sites that connect to M-Pesa through Safaricom's Daraja API, writes Python, and cleans and analyses data in Excel.

Alongside the newsroom, he offers website development, online marketing and SEO services. See his work at winslausokhala.github.io. Where a story promotes those services, we say so.
