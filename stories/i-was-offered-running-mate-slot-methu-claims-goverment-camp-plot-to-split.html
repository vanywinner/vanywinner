“I Was Offered Running Mate Slot”: Methu Claims Goverment Camp Plot to Split Opposition

News

By Newsroom, 12 Oct, 12:50

NAIROBI: Nyandarua Senator John Methu has made explosive claims of a plot to split the opposition ahead of the 2027 General Election. He alleges that people linked to the government offered to bankroll a political party for him, and he has turned on a close ally who he says promised to join the opposition.

Speaking on Citizen TV on Sunday, Methu said individuals from the government approached him with a proposal to form a political party. He said they would finance the party and its rallies in private while publicly appearing to oppose him. According to Methu, the two sides would later converge, and he would be named President William Ruto’s running mate.

The senator said he rejected the offer and called Kiharu MP Ndindi Nyoro, whom he regards as a friend, to tell him about it. Methu said Nyoro advised him not to fall for the trap, warning that a new party would divide the united opposition. He said Nyoro also told him he would soon join the Democracy for Citizens Party (DCP) and be part of the opposition.

Methu said that is not what happened. To his surprise, Nyoro formed his own political party, which he said contradicted their discussions and agreements. “If I was to name moles, then I think the name Ndindi Nyoro will feature prominently,” Methu said.

The claims come as Mount Kenya’s politics shifts ahead of next year’s polls. Nyoro, once a close Ruto ally, has become a vocal critic of the government and is the subject of speculation about presidential ambitions.

Methu did not name the officials who approached him and offered no evidence for the alleged offer. Nyoro and State House had not responded to the claims at the time of publishing.
