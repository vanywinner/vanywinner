Kenyan National Indicted in US Over $20,000 Student Aid Fraud Scheme

Business

By Vanywinner KE, 30 Oct, 12:33

A Kenyan national, Francis Mogambi, has been indicted in the United States over allegations of stealing American identities to fraudulently obtain federal student financial aid.

The US Department of Justice said Mogambi was indicted by a federal grand jury in Nebraska on October 19. He faces five counts of theft of government money and five counts of aggravated identity theft.

Prosecutors allege that between November 2025 and May 2026, Mogambi worked with associates in Kenya to use stolen identities to enrol fictitious students and obtain federal financial aid. He allegedly retained 30 per cent of the proceeds and transferred the remainder to his associates.

The alleged loss in the Nebraska case is approximately $20,000. Mogambi is also accused of offering another person $2,000 a week to participate in the scheme.

The case is linked to Operation Ghostbusters, a wider US investigation into suspected fraud involving stolen identities and fictitious student accounts.

Mogambi is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty in court.

Source: US Department of Justice
