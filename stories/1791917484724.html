Meet “Yesu wa Bombolulu”

News

By Newsroom, 13 Oct, 21:51

A man known on social media as “Yesu wa Bombolulu”, meaning “Jesus of Bombolulu”, has allegedly been arrested in Mombasa after videos showed him leading a crowd through the streets. The clips have drawn curiosity and mixed reactions from Kenyans online.

According to accounts circulating online, the man has fed congregants at gatherings and has also chased people out of church. On the day he was allegedly arrested, he was reportedly on his way to a hospital on what he described as a mission to heal the sick. However, his real name, age and church affiliation, if any, have not been made public in the reports seen so far.

Meanwhile, the videos of the incident have spread across TikTok and Threads. Some viewers found the scenes amusing, while others voiced concern. Some commenters even suggested that local content creators were having fun with residents, although that claim has not been verified.

He is not the first self-styled messiah to attract police attention in Kenya. In May 2023, Eliud Wekesa, popularly known as “Yesu wa Tongaren”, was arrested during a state crackdown on church activities. A court in Bungoma later ordered a mental assessment and allowed police to hold him for four more days to complete investigations.

For now, many questions remain unanswered. A police statement confirming the arrest had not been found at the time of writing, and it is unclear where the man is being held, whether he has been charged, or what the reason for the arrest was.

This story will be updated as more details emerge.
