Trump Faces Fresh Impeachment Threat as Democrats Seek House Control

News

By Newsroom, 4 Nov, 3:08

President Donald Trump could face a third impeachment attempt if Democrats regain control of the US House of Representatives following Tuesday's midterm elections.

A Democratic victory would give the party control of congressional committees, opening the door to investigations into Trump's administration, business dealings and executive decisions.

House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries has left the possibility of impeachment open, saying the party would be guided by facts, the law and the Constitution.

Trump has previously been impeached twice, in 2019 and 2021, but was acquitted by the Senate on both occasions.

While a Democratic-controlled House could approve new articles of impeachment, removing Trump from office would require a two-thirds majority in the Senate, a significant political hurdle.

Democrats remain divided over whether impeachment should be a priority, with some lawmakers favouring investigations and congressional oversight instead.

The election outcome could therefore reshape Trump's final two years in office, potentially placing his administration under sustained scrutiny even if a third impeachment does not proceed.
