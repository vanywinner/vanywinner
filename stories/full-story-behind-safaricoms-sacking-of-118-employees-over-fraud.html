Full Story Behind Safaricom’s Sacking of 118 Employees Over Fraud

Business

By Vanywinner KE, 28 Oct, 9:17

Safaricom has disclosed that it dismissed 118 officials over fraud-related cases during the financial year that ended in March 2026, while M-Pesa processed transactions worth KSh 41.68 trillion over the same period.

The details are contained in the telecommunications firm's Business and Sustainability Report for the 2025/2026 financial year, which outlines the company's response to fraud, disciplinary measures taken against employees and the scale of transactions processed through its mobile money platform.

According to the report, Safaricom issued three disciplinary warnings and referred 14 cases to law enforcement agencies for further action. The dismissals formed part of the company's measures to address fraud-related incidents and strengthen accountability within its operations.

The report also indicates that Safaricom recorded an 82 per cent reduction in fraud-related cases, against a target of 90 per cent. However, the company reportedly recovered 46 per cent of the items or assets reported lost, highlighting the continuing challenges associated with fraud prevention and recovery.

Beyond the fraud disclosures, the report provides an indication of the scale of financial activity taking place through M-Pesa, one of Kenya's most widely used mobile money platforms.

During the financial year under review, M-Pesa processed approximately 46.4 billion transactions, with the total value reaching KSh 41.68 trillion. The figures underscore the platform's central role in Kenya's financial ecosystem, where individuals and businesses rely on mobile money for payments, transfers and other financial services.

The disclosures offer insight into Safaricom's efforts to combat fraud while managing the enormous volume of financial transactions passing through its systems. They also highlight the importance of internal controls, accountability and cooperation with law enforcement in protecting customers and the company from fraud-related risks.
