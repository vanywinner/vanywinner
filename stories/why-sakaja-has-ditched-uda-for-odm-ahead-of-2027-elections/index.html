Why Sakaja Has Ditched UDA for ODM Ahead of 2027 Elections

News

By Vanywinner KE, 2 Nov, 14:11

Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja has made a significant political move by ditching the United Democratic Alliance (UDA) for the Orange Democratic Movement (ODM), setting the stage for a fresh contest ahead of the 2027 General Election.

Sakaja’s decision is widely viewed as a strategic attempt to strengthen his chances of retaining the Nairobi governorship, a seat that has become increasingly competitive as political parties begin positioning themselves for the next election.

Nairobi remains one of Kenya’s most politically influential counties, with ODM enjoying considerable support among voters. By joining the party, Sakaja could be seeking to align himself with the electorate’s political preferences while expanding his support base beyond his previous UDA affiliation.

The governor’s relationship with the late former Prime Minister Raila Odinga has also featured prominently in discussions surrounding his political transition. Sakaja has previously described Raila as a father figure and acknowledged the influence he had on his political journey. His move to ODM could therefore reflect both political calculations and personal political associations.

The decision also comes at a time when UDA is seeking to consolidate its influence in Nairobi and across the country. Sakaja’s departure could complicate the party’s efforts to maintain a strong presence in the capital, particularly as the 2027 campaigns begin taking shape.

However, joining ODM does not automatically guarantee Sakaja the party’s gubernatorial ticket. The governor could face competition from other influential leaders within the party, including Makadara MP George Aladwa, who has previously expressed interest in the Nairobi governorship.

Sakaja’s political shift therefore presents both an opportunity and a challenge. While ODM could offer him a stronger electoral platform, securing the party’s nomination and convincing Nairobi voters to support his re-election bid will remain critical.

His move could also reshape Nairobi’s political alliances, making the county one of the key battlegrounds to watch in the run-up to the 2027 General Election.
