Trump Weighs Fresh Iran Strikes as US Military Prepares for Possible War Resumption

News

By Vanywinner KE, 6 Nov, 11:35

US President Donald Trump is considering a fresh wave of military strikes against Iran after holding high-level security meetings at the White House, raising fears of a renewed escalation in the Middle East.

Senior US officials confirmed that Trump met with his national security team on Wednesday and Thursday to discuss whether Washington should resume major military operations against Tehran. According to reports, the US Central Command (CENTCOM) is finalising preparations for a possible operation, although no formal order to launch new strikes has been issued.

Speaking to reporters at the White House, Trump warned that further military action remained an option as Washington seeks to prevent Iran from reviving its nuclear programme.

“I would rather not destroy the rest of the country if possible, but maybe I will have to. You’ll know soon enough,” Trump said, signalling that a decision could come soon.

The latest developments come amid growing uncertainty over the future of the US-Iran conflict, with Washington weighing military action against continued economic pressure and diplomatic negotiations. Regional mediators, including Qatar, are reportedly working to prevent a renewed confrontation, while Tehran has maintained conditions for further negotiations.

The possibility of fresh strikes has raised concerns about the wider consequences for regional security, global energy supplies and shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, a critical route for international oil trade.

Despite the military preparations, the White House has not announced a final decision. The coming days could determine whether Washington pursues another major offensive or attempts to secure a diplomatic agreement with Tehran.
