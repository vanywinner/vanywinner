Sakaja Sacks Mosiria Days After Declaring Nairobi Governorship Bid

News

By Vanywinner KE, 21 Oct, 17:33

Nairobi Governor Johnson Sakaja has removed Geoffrey Mosiria from his position as Chief Officer for Public Participation, Citizen Engagement and Customer Service, days after Mosiria announced his intention to contest the governorship in the 2027 General Election.

The decision was communicated through a circular dated Wednesday, October 21, 2026, signed by Nairobi County Secretary and Head of Public Service Dr Machel Waikenda. The circular, referenced NCC/CS/003/2026, outlines changes to the responsibilities of senior county officials, with the new arrangements taking effect immediately.

Under the changes, Zipporah Njeri Mwangi will serve as Acting Chief Officer for Public Participation, Citizen Engagement and Customer Service while retaining her existing responsibilities in the City Culture, Arts and Tourism docket. Eng. Samwel Njogu has been appointed Acting Chief Officer for Mobility, while Godfrey Akumali has also been assigned new responsibilities.

The circular cites Sections 44(3)(a), 45(5) and 64 of the County Governments Act, 2012, as the legal basis for the administrative changes. However, it does not provide reasons for Mosiria's removal or indicate whether he has been assigned another role within the county government.

The development comes ten days after Mosiria publicly announced his intention to challenge Sakaja for the Nairobi governorship in the 2027 General Election.

Mosiria made the announcement on October 11 while addressing worshippers at Jesus Is Alive Ministries during a service hosted by Bishop Margaret Wanjiru. He cited his experience in health, environmental management and citizen engagement as preparation for the county's top position, pledging to address challenges affecting service delivery in Nairobi.

His declaration followed reports of disagreements with the governor over the management of county affairs. In an interview on October 19, Mosiria said he had been sidelined following differences over the use of public funds and the delivery of services.

He also indicated that he intended to remain in his county government position until the official campaign period began.

On Wednesday, Mosiria made further allegations on social media, claiming that Sakaja and Embakasi East MP Babu Owino were working together against him. He described himself as their “common enemy.”

Neither Nairobi City Hall nor representatives of Owino had responded to the claims by the time of reporting.

Mosiria also said he was aware of previous legal attempts to challenge his position and vowed to defend himself against allegations he considers false and malicious.

Mosiria has served in several senior positions within the Nairobi County Government since Sakaja took office following the 2022 General Election. He was initially appointed Chief Officer for Health before moving to the Environment docket, where he became known for leading enforcement operations targeting illegal dumping and other environmental violations.

In November 2025, he was transferred to the Public Participation, Citizen Engagement and Customer Service docket as part of an earlier administrative reshuffle.

His removal marks another change in the county government's senior management as political activity gradually intensifies ahead of the 2027 elections.

Mosiria has not announced whether he intends to challenge the decision in court. He has, however, maintained that his ambition to contest the Nairobi governorship remains unchanged.

The circumstances surrounding his removal, including whether the county government will issue a further explanation and whether Mosiria will pursue legal action, remain key issues to watch as the political contest for Nairobi's top seat develops.
