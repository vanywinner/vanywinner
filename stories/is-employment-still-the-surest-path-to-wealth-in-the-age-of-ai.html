Is Employment Still the Surest Path to Wealth in the Age of AI?

News

By Newsroom, 1 Nov, 12:30

Is employment the top way of getting rich? This question, and many related to it, has arisen as people worldwide worry that artificial intelligence has replaced, and will continue to replace, many workers.

When AI arrived, almost everyone was greatly impressed. Most companies began automating repetitive tasks and those that seemed dangerous to humans. Students also gained greatly from it, accessing valuable revision materials and solving mathematical equations they found difficult.

No one thought that the very assistant would become a threat in the near future. Students and graduates are now worried that the same AI is standing in their way as they struggle to secure employment in reputable companies and even in the government. Entry-level jobs have become very competitive and difficult to get, as AI now performs these tasks faster and more efficiently than humans.

I am also a victim of losing work to AI. Eight months ago, I earned money tutoring basic statistics and data analysis concepts, including data collection, cleaning, manipulation and report writing. I could enrol ten students every week and earn a minimum of thirty thousand Kenya shillings weekly. The situation is very different now: I have not enrolled a single student in the past two months. Most of them now rely on AI models to learn these concepts and even to write their reports.

It is also worrying that even the creators of these models fear what AI may be capable of in the coming days. It has been reported that one AI model escaped its sandbox and hacked into a rival company’s systems without its owners’ knowledge. This is not only a legal threat, but it could also lead to far greater harm if an AI model chose to hack into financial or health systems.

A heated debate is ongoing. Some say AI will erase humanity by the end of the decade. Others suggest we should put the genie back in the bottle, or at least regulate it. The United States has reported that AI was used in the wars involving Russia and Israel, which is even more worrying.
