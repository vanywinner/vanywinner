ICT Authority Opens Web Design Training for 40 Professionals and Students

Business

By Newsroom, 12 Oct, 10:56

The training, run with CAP Youth Empowerment Institute Kenya, will take place from October 21 to November 5, 2026. Sessions run from 5:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m., an evening slot meant to suit working professionals and students who cannot attend during the day. It is part of the Citizens Digital Skills Programme, under the national Training 20 Million Citizens initiative.

Participants will get hands-on practice with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, the core tools behind most websites. The organizers say the aim is for graduates to build responsive, real-world websites that work across phones, tablets and desktop computers. Trainees will also join the Smart Academy tech community, which could help them find collaborators, mentors and job leads after the course ends.

The timing reflects growing demand for web skills in Kenya’s economy. Small businesses, schools and service providers increasingly need an online presence to reach customers, and many cannot afford large agency fees. For students and young professionals, a short practical course can be a low-cost way to turn a skill into freelance income or a stronger job application.

Space is limited to 40 slots. The ICT Authority says registration closes on October 20, 2026, though a promotional poster for the same course gives October 18, so applicants should register early. Interested applicants should register through the official Smart Academy portal at smartacademy.go.ke, and find further information on the ICT Authority website at icta.go.ke. The ICT Authority says questions can go to capacity.dev@ict.go.ke or citizens@icta.go.ke. The announcement does not state whether the training carries a fee, and Smart Academy’s course catalog lists its standard web design course at Ksh 5,000, so applicants should confirm the cost with the organizers before registering.
