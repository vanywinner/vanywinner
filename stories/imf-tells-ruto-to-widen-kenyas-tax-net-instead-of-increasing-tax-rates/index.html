IMF Tells Ruto to Widen Kenya's Tax Net Instead of Increasing Tax Rates

News

By Newsroom, 2 Nov, 14:47

The International Monetary Fund (IMF) has urged President William Ruto's administration to broaden Kenya's tax base rather than rely on increasing tax rates to raise government revenue.

In comments reported on November 2, 2026, IMF Communications Director Julie Kozack highlighted the importance of improving domestic revenue collection as Kenya continues to deal with mounting public debt and pressure on government finances.

The recommendation means the government should focus on bringing more eligible individuals and businesses into the tax system, improving compliance and reviewing tax exemptions that limit revenue collection.

The IMF's position comes amid growing public debate over taxation and the rising cost of living, with many Kenyans expressing concerns about the financial burden placed on households and businesses.

Broadening the tax base does not necessarily mean introducing new taxes. Instead, it involves ensuring that existing tax obligations are fairly distributed and properly enforced.

The recommendation presents the government with a challenge of increasing revenue while balancing economic growth and the financial pressures facing ordinary Kenyans.
