1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website saves on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in the same way. We use them to remember your choices and, only if you agree, to measure how the site is used.

3. Your choices

  • On our site. The first time you visit, a banner asks you to accept all cookies or keep only essential ones. Analytics and advertising cookies are not loaded unless you accept.
  • Change your mind. Select here or in the footer of any page to choose again.
  • In your browser. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may mean we ask for your choice again on every visit.
  • Advertising. You can manage personalised ads in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out through aboutads.info.
  • Google Analytics opt-out. Google offers a browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
