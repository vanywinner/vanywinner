Share your opinion with Vanywinner News

We publish guest opinions on trending topics and on any subject that matters to Kenyans. If you have a clear argument, write it here. Every submission is read by our editor before anything is published.

What we look for

  • An original piece written by you, between 150 and 2,500 words (300 to 1,000 works best).
  • One clear point of view, supported by facts, examples or experience. Name your sources where you use figures or quotes.
  • A fair tone. We do not publish hate speech, personal attacks, unproven allegations against named people, or content that promotes violence.
  • No copied text and no articles already published elsewhere.

What happens next

  • Your article goes to our editor as pending review. It is not public until it is approved.
  • We may edit for length, clarity and legal safety. We may contact you if we have questions, which is why an email or phone number is helpful, though both are optional.
  • Your name appears on the published article. Your email and phone number are never published.
  • Opinions are the views of their authors and do not necessarily reflect those of Vanywinner News. We cannot promise that every article will be published.
